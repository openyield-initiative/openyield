How to read this demonstration

In the default demonstration, A starts in the passing space. B passes, then A slides sideways and proceeds. These are scripted actions. Rectangles show the exact modeled footprints; this is not a steering model, negotiation method or evidence of real-world safety.

Recording view keeps the composition above in 16:9, with the caveat always visible. Use an existing screen recorder to capture that region. On a phone, use the normal layout for reading; record in landscape for legible video.

Positions are in metres. Playback shows discrete recorded frames; speed controls change viewing speed only. Returning from a hidden tab stays paused.
